The Full Story

Why SellTru Exists

Andrew Deramo
Founder & Head of Strategy
Amazon SPN Certified Amazon Ads Verified Walmart Partner Upwork Top Rated Plus

Before I ran an agency, I was the client. I was a business owner trying to grow on marketplaces, and I did what every brand owner does when they hit a ceiling: I hired help. Big-name agencies, polished pitch decks, confident promises. I sat through the kickoff calls, signed the retainers, and waited.

What I got back were reports full of impressions and clicks while my actual numbers stayed flat. The person who sold me disappeared the day the contract was signed, and my account went to whoever had bandwidth that month. I didn't just hear about the broken agency model — I paid for it, more than once.

So I learned it myself

I stopped outsourcing and went deep on how Amazon actually works — campaign structure, match types, ranking, the unglamorous weekly optimization work that agencies skip when nobody's watching. That was more than seven years ago. Since then I've managed over $150M in marketplace sales across 150+ Amazon brands and 60+ Walmart brands, and earned the certifications that can't be bought: Amazon SPN, Amazon Ads Verified Partner, Walmart Marketplace Partner, and Upwork Top Rated Plus.

Why we only do marketplaces

Amazon and Walmart change too fast to be a side offering. An agency that also runs your social ads, your email, and your website redesign is an agency that's average at all of it. We picked one thing — Amazon and Walmart.com — and built the entire company around being better at it than anyone else.

How SellTru is run differently

I'm personally in every account we take on. Not "executive oversight" — actually in the campaigns, looking at the same dashboards my team works in. We stay deliberately small, we only take brands we know we can move, and we price so that the relationship only works if you grow. That's the agency I was looking for as a business owner and never found. So I built it.
