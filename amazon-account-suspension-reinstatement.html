Do you guarantee reinstatement?

+

No. Anyone who guarantees it is selling hope. We build a complete Plan of Action and follow Amazon's process. Amazon decides.

How long does an appeal take?

+

It varies by queue and reason. Some decisions return in days. Others take weeks. We do not invent a timeline Amazon did not publish.

Account suspension vs listing suspension?

+

Account suspension blocks selling. Listing suspension hits one or more ASINs while the account may still operate. Different evidence and urgency.

Can you help after a rejected appeal?

+

Often yes, if there is new evidence or a clearer root cause. Repeating the same letter without changes usually fails again.

Will you take over the account afterward?

+

If you want ongoing management, yes, under a separate retainer. Reinstatement alone does not require a long-term contract.

What cases do you decline?

+

Fraud, review manipulation schemes, and fabricated documents. We will not file those. Better to hear that on the audit than after a ban.
