FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does Amazon advertising management include?

Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, and Sponsored Display, plus Amazon DSP when the account is ready. Weekly search term work, bids, budgets, negatives, and a written note. Listing and inventory sit in the same plan when those retainers are active.

Who is Amazon advertising management for?

Brands doing $1M to $20M that want a person in the account every week. Below that range we are usually not the right fit.

How does pricing work?

Flat monthly retainers from $3K. Ad spend is paid to Amazon and is separate. We do not take a percent of spend as the default model. See marketplace agency pricing for ranges.

How is this different from Amazon PPC management?

PPC management is the Sponsored Ads core. This page covers the broader advertising stack, including when DSP joins. Same operators. See Amazon PPC management for the PPC-only scope.

Do you work Seller Central and Vendor Central?

Both. Vendor ads are scoped when you sell 1P. See Vendor Central management.

What results should I expect?

Across our managed book, average ACOS drop of 45% in 90 days when ads are in scope. That is a book average, not a promise for every ASIN. $150M+ sales managed, 200+ brands, 90% retention.
