Pricing from a Fort Lauderdale team

The fee does not change because you can drive here. SellTru uses a flat monthly retainer, and published retainers start at $3,000 a month. The market range we documented is $1,500 to $8,000 a month, depending on whether you are buying ads-only help or full channel management. Percent-of-spend pricing is common in the market and we do not use it. Read the Amazon agency pricing guide before you compare a local quote with a national one. If the other quote is a percent of ad spend, you are comparing different incentives, not just different cities.

Ad spend paid to Amazon is separate from the retainer. You keep approval on budget increases. We are not cheaper because the office is in Florida, and we are not a luxury add-on for brands that want a downtown address on a proposal. The address is where the work is run.
