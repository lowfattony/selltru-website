Do I need Brand Registry?

+

Yes for almost all protection tools. Without Registry you cannot use Report a Violation or Project Zero the way brand owners expect.

Can you guarantee a hijacker is removed?

+

No. Amazon decides. We build complete filings and follow up. Outcomes vary by evidence and Amazon's queue.

Is this the same as reinstatement?

+

No. Protection tries to keep you out of trouble. Reinstatement is for after a suspension. Different page, different process.

Do you handle counterfeit cases?

+

Yes when you have Brand Registry and clear evidence. Counterfeit claims without proof get rejected and can hurt the account.

MAP enforcement?

+

We document offers and use the channels Amazon provides. We are not a law firm and do not send cease-and-desist letters as counsel.

Pricing?
