What is FBA management?

+

The operating work around Fulfillment by Amazon: forecasting, inbound shipments, storage fees, stranded units, and reimbursements. It is not a substitute for your 3PL's warehouse labor.

Do you create shipment plans?

+

Yes. We build and monitor FBA inbound shipments in Seller Central, including carton labeling guidance and receiving follow-ups.

Can you recover lost inventory?

+

When Amazon's records support a reimbursement, we file it. Not every discrepancy pays out. We do not promise a recovery dollar amount up front.

How does this work with PPC?

+

The same team sees both. We do not scale a campaign into a known stockout window. That is part of account management, not a handoff email.

Do you manage WFS too?

+

Yes for brands on both marketplaces. Walmart Fulfillment Services has its own page at Walmart WFS management.

Pricing?

+

Flat monthly retainers from $3K when FBA sits inside full account management. A narrower ops scope can be quoted on the audit. Ad spend is separate.
