Do you replace my vendor manager?

+

No. We operate alongside Amazon's vendor managers. We prepare the numbers and the catalog work so those conversations are shorter and clearer.

Seller Central or Vendor Central?

+

Depends on the brand. Many of our clients are 3P-first. Vendor Central is right when 1P distribution and Amazon retail demand outweigh the margin tradeoff.

Can you reduce chargebacks?

+

We reduce avoidable ones with PO discipline and content fixes, and we dispute the rest when evidence exists. We do not promise a zero chargeback month.

+

Yes. Vendor advertising is part of the retainer when scoped. Spend is paid to Amazon and is separate from the retainer.

Hybrid accounts?
