Amazon Established Consumer Brand

$617,000 in a Single Month on Amazon

This was a brand that had been on Amazon for several years but had never broken through to consistent high-revenue months. Their account had layers of campaigns built up over time by multiple people, with no clear structure tying it together. Good products. Unrealized potential.

When we audited the account, we found significant wasted spend in campaigns that had never converted, and strong-performing keywords buried in campaigns with budgets too low to do anything meaningful.

What we did

  • Full campaign restructure — organized by SKU with branded, non-branded, and auto campaigns separated
  • Identified top-converting keywords and shifted budget toward them with properly segmented match types
  • Cut wasted spend in auto campaigns that had been running without negative keyword harvesting
  • Launched Sponsored Brands campaigns targeting category-level keywords to capture upper-funnel traffic
  • Optimized listing copy and A+ content to improve conversion rate on traffic already arriving

Amazon Health & Wellness Brand

ACoS From 49% to 43% — While Sales Grew 18%

This brand came to us with Amazon ads that were generating sales but bleeding margin. Their ACoS was sitting at 49% — well above the threshold where most products can be profitable after COGS and fees. The instinct is to just cut budgets. That's almost never the right move.

The real problem was structural. Branded, non-branded, and auto campaigns were all mixed together in the same campaigns. There was no way to read the data accurately, let alone optimize it.

What we did

  • Rebuilt campaign structure from scratch — organized by individual SKU within the first few days
  • Separated exact match, phrase match, and broad match into their own campaigns for clean bid control
  • Harvested converting search terms from auto campaigns and promoted them to manual with correct bids
  • Identified and cut keywords with high spend and zero conversions over a 60-day window
  • Preserved and increased budget on what was already working — not a full reset

Our Process
