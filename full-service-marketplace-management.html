FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is full-service marketplace management?

One SellTru team running ads, listings, and account operations for Amazon and Walmart under a single plan. Weekly console work, not a software handoff.

Who is this for?

Brands doing $1M to $20M that want both marketplaces managed together. Below that range we are usually not the right fit. Single-channel needs map to our Amazon or Walmart service pages.

How does pricing work?

Flat monthly retainers from $3K. Ad spend is paid to Amazon or Walmart and is separate. We do not take a percent of spend as the default model. See marketplace agency pricing.

Do I have to run both Amazon and Walmart?

No. Many brands start on one channel. Full-service framing is ready when the second channel is live or launching. We still scope Amazon-only or Walmart-only retainers on the dedicated pages.

What proof can you share?

$150M+ sales managed, 200+ brands, and 90% retention. On Amazon ads accounts in scope, average ACOS drop of 45% in 90 days. That is a book average, not a promise for every ASIN.

How is this different from Amazon account management?

Amazon account management is the Amazon-only hub. This page is the dual-channel full-service offer. Same company, different scope page.
