What Walmart A+ content actually is

Walmart files the work under rich media. Two pieces matter for most brands:

  • Brand Highlights. Image, video, and text layouts on the product detail page. Built in Seller Center Media Library, then attached to GTINs you own. Brand Portal enrollment is required.
  • Listing video. Product video available more broadly than Brand Highlights. Still uploaded through the Media Library, with file and category limits Walmart enforces at submit time.

People still type "Walmart A+ content" because they know Amazon A+. The shopper job is the same. The module set, the eligibility rules, and the upload path are not. We design for Walmart instead of exporting Amazon A+ and hoping the Media Library accepts it.

Brand Portal eligibility first

Brand Highlights are for brand owners in Brand Portal with an active USPTO trademark, a clean IP and Trust and Safety record, and published new-condition items they own. Restricted product types, including alcohol, tobacco, and firearms, cannot use certain rich media. If Registry-style access is the blocker, that is the first project, not a design sprint.

We confirm rights in the account before we open a layout. Designing modules a brand cannot attach wastes a week and a retainer conversation.

Our design process

  1. Read Walmart reviews and Q&A first. The module list comes from objections, not from a folder of lifestyle photos left over from Amazon.
  2. Read the Walmart title and key features. Brand Highlights should extend them, not restate them in a larger font.
  3. Check Brand Portal and item ownership. No design work starts until the GTIN can actually receive the layout.
  4. Design for phone first. Most of the scroll on Walmart.com happens there. Type that only works on a desktop mock fails the visit.
  5. Upload in Media Library, then attach. Prebuilt templates or a custom layout, then link to eligible items. Fix what Walmart rejects for policy or file rules.

Module strategy that converts

Every block has one job. A comparison layout is for the shopper choosing between sizes or between your line and the obvious alternative, and the facts in it have to be true. A brand story is why the product exists, in one pass, not a company timeline. A lifestyle frame is the use the reviews keep describing. If a module does not answer a hesitation, it comes out.

Video earns its place when the product needs to be shown in use: a scoop, a setup order, a fit on a body, a kit in sequence. A static hero with more marketing adjectives does not need a clip attached to it.

We do not invent lift numbers. Walmart says rich media can help engagement, conversions, and returns. It does not publish a universal percentage we can put in a pitch deck. Amazon publishes its own A+ ceilings on the Amazon A+ content page. Those are Amazon's numbers, not a Walmart forecast, and not a SellTru case study for your GTIN.

Walmart A+ and Walmart Connect together

Brand Highlights do not fix a traffic problem. If the item has no views, the work belongs on Walmart Connect ads, on search attributes, and on reviews. If the item has views and no orders, the modules and the primary images are usually the missing piece. One team owns both so the ad click and the page tell the same story.

Fulfillment still matters. A beautiful Brand Highlights block on an item that loses the Buy Box to a faster WFS offer is decoration. Inventory and inbound sit on Walmart WFS management.

FAQ

Does Walmart have A+ content?

Yes. Shoppers and brands often call it Walmart A+ content. On Walmart.com the product is Brand Highlights, plus listing video, under rich media in Seller Center. Brand Highlights combine images, video, and text on the product detail page. Video is available more broadly. Brand Highlights require Brand Portal enrollment and brand-owner rights on the items you attach.

Who can publish Walmart Brand Highlights?

Brand owners registered in Walmart Brand Portal, with an active USPTO trademark, no recorded IP or Trust and Safety violation, and published new-condition items they own. Restricted categories such as alcohol, tobacco, and firearms cannot use certain rich media. We check eligibility in the account before we design modules you cannot submit.

How is Walmart A+ different from Amazon A+?

The shopper job is similar: answer objections below the buy box with image, copy, and video. The tooling is not. Walmart Brand Highlights live in the Media Library, attach to GTINs you own, and sit behind Brand Portal rules. Amazon A+ sits behind Brand Registry and Amazon's module set. We design for each channel instead of pasting Amazon modules into Walmart.

How much do Walmart A+ content services cost?

We do not publish a separate design fee on this page. Brand Highlights and rich media work is scoped on the same flat retainer as the rest of the Walmart listing work, or as a one-time pass if that is all you need. Ad spend is paid to Walmart Connect and is separate. Retainer models are on the pricing guide.

How long does Walmart Brand Highlights take?

Design follows the listing pass. Upload and attach happen in Seller Center Media Library once Brand Portal rights are in place. We do not promise same-day publish. A missing trademark, a Trust and Safety flag, or an item that is not brand-owned will stop the attach step, so we clear those before production.

Will Brand Highlights lift my Walmart conversion rate?

Walmart says rich media such as videos and Brand Highlights can help engagement, conversions, and returns. It does not publish a universal lift percentage we can quote. We treat modules as conversion work for items that already get views, not as a substitute for search rank, reviews, or fulfillment. If the click is the problem, the work belongs on Walmart Connect.

Channel strategy beyond the modules sits on Walmart marketplace agency. Brands that also need Amazon modules start on Amazon A+ content.
