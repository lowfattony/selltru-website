What is WFS?

+

Walmart Fulfillment Services. Walmart stores and ships your inventory to customers. Fees and lead times differ from Amazon FBA.

Who should use WFS?

+

Brands with steady Walmart demand that need two-day speed and want Walmart to handle last-mile. Low-velocity SKUs often stay seller-fulfilled.

Do you compare FBA and WFS?

+

Yes. Fee math, lead times, and which channel should carry each SKU. We do not move inventory for its own sake.

Does WFS help search rank?

+

Availability and fulfillment speed are ranking inputs. Stockouts hurt. WFS is one path to stay in stock, not a rank guarantee.

Is Connect included?

+

When scoped. Ads and WFS share inventory signals. See Walmart Connect ads.

Pricing?
